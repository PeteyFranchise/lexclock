How it works

From your real work to a billed entry — in three steps.

LexClock watches where billable work actually happens and does the timekeeping you'd never get around to. You stay the final word on every line.

1

It captures the work

Connect your email and task tools. LexClock reads the activity — who, what matter, how long — without you starting a timer or writing a note.

Security & data use

Your inbox is privileged. We treat it that way.

LexClock requests the minimum access needed to draft time entries, and never more. Here's exactly how your data is used.

Read-only, scoped access

We read message metadata and content only to draft time entries. LexClock never sends, deletes, or modifies your email — access is read-only.

Encrypted & never sold

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell client data or use privileged content to train third-party models. You can revoke access anytime.

You stay in control

Nothing is billed without your explicit approval. Disconnect a source or delete your data in one click — and it's gone for good.
