Brand & Design System · v1

The system behind a billing assistant lawyers actually trust.

LexClock captures every billable minute automatically — and never bills a thing the attorney hasn't reviewed and approved. This sheet is the visual contract that keeps it feeling precise, calm, and legal-grade.

Built to live comfortably beside Clio in the marketplace, while standing as its own mark. Ocean blue for trust, one disciplined orange for action, white for clarity.

Principle 01

Nothing bills without approval

Every surface keeps the lawyer in control. Review, confidence, consent — the orange accent only ever means “go.”

Principle 02

Always show the evidence

Transparency over magic. Each suggestion carries the source and the “why,” as first-class evidence chips.

Principle 03

Trustworthy, precise, modern

Legal-grade credibility — not flashy SaaS. Spacious, high-contrast, quietly modern. Confident, never loud.

01

Logo & the mark

LexClock = lex (law) + clock (time/billing). The mark fuses a clock with an approval gesture. Below: the primary lockup, five directions to choose from, and the wordmark study. Every mark holds up in one color and at favicon scale.

LexClock

Primary lockup — “Approval Clock.” A clock whose hands rest at an angle that reads as a checkmark; the long “approval” hand is the brand’s single orange. Use this everywhere there’s room.

Wordmark study

Primary · Libre Franklin 800
LexClock

Default wordmark. Humanist sans, tight tracking, two-tone weight to mark the lex/clock seam without a literal break. Reads cleanly in nav bars and against Clio’s marketplace.

Gravitas variant · Source Serif 4
LexClock

Optional serif treatment for formal moments — invoices, letterhead, the homepage masthead — where a touch of legal gravitas helps. Same color logic.

Clear space & minimum size

LexClock

Keep clear space equal to the height of the clock mark on every side. Never set the wordmark below 20px tall; below that, switch to the icon-only mark (which holds down to 16px).
